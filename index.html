Action Plan

Where BabyBuddha Can Improve

Checked against BabyBuddha's live website before writing these — each one flags whether relevant content already exists or is a genuine gap.

01
Genuine gap — new content

Build a core “Breastfeeding & Nursing Basics” guide (latch, let-down, colostrum)

This is the single largest-volume tracked topic (59,840/mo) and BabyBuddha holds zero of its 50 tracked citations. The site has related posts (breastfeeding myths, diet, 5 tips for new moms) but no dedicated fundamentals guide covering latch technique, let-down and colostrum — checked via on-site search, which surfaces only tangential content for this topic. This is genuinely new content, not a retarget.

02
Existing content — retarget

Retarget BabyBuddha's wearable-pump content around “hands-free” and “wearable breast pump” queries

Wearable / In-Bra Pumps is the second-largest tracked topic (45,500/mo) and BabyBuddha currently holds zero citations while Lansinoh, Momcozy, Spectra, Zomee and Pumpables each hold at least one. BabyBuddha already has dedicated wearable content live — the wearable-pump launch post, “Primary vs Wearable Breast Pumps,” “BabyBuddha 2.0 vs BabyBuddha Wearable” and “BabyBuddha vs Willow” — confirmed via on-site search. This needs a headline/structure retarget toward the exact “hands-free”/“wearable” phrasing AI engines are citing competitors for, not new writing.

03
Existing content — retarget

Restructure the “Best Breast Pumps of 2026” roundup to win more Buying-Guide citations

Pump Reviews & Buying Guides citations are currently led by Pumpables (3 of 5 tracked) despite Pumpables being the smallest brand in this set, with BabyBuddha at 1. BabyBuddha already publishes “The Best Breast Pumps of 2026: Mom-Tested and Expert-Approved” — confirmed live on-site — so this is a retarget of an existing roundup's structure and headline toward comparison-style AI queries, not a new page.

04
Existing content — retarget

Retarget the existing milk-storage guide toward AI Overview/AI Mode citation queries

Milk Storage, Freezing & Spoilage carries real tracked volume (4,380/mo across 24 prompts) and BabyBuddha holds zero citations while Momcozy, Zomee and Pumpables each hold several. BabyBuddha already has “Breast Milk Storage Guidelines: Fridge, Freezer, Thawing and Safety” live on-site — an exact topical match confirmed via on-site search — so the opportunity is restructuring that existing page (clearer Q&A formatting, direct answers up front) rather than writing something new.

05
Genuine gap — new content

Launch a structured digital-PR push around DME/insurance and lactation-resource partners

BabyBuddha's named-brand mention volume across AI engines is far behind this set's leaders (144 vs. Momcozy's 8,573 and eufy's 5,348), and its distinct citation-source breadth is narrower than the top brands. aeroflowbreastpumps.com — an insurance/DME retailer and resource hub — is already BabyBuddha's top neutral third-party co-citation partner (10 co-citations, see Spotlight above), suggesting that category of partner is already primed to carry BabyBuddha's name further. A recurring placement push with DME/insurance and lactation-resource sites compounds brand-mention and source-coverage gains across every topic at once, rather than one topic at a time.
